Technology

The power of a capacitor with the energy of a battery.

UltraCap uses lithium-carbon capacitor cells, an asymmetric (hybrid) supercapacitor. Charge is stored mainly by ions clinging to the electrode surface (ion adsorption), with only shallow insertion into the electrode material. The electrodes barely wear, so the cells can charge and discharge at very high rates for an exceptionally long life.

  • 100,000 cycles at cell, pack and system level
  • 15-year calendar life with maintenance-free cells
  • Full operation from −40 °C to 60 °C, so it suits cold and hot climates alike
  • Low self-discharge: ≤ 2% per month at module level
