Why sodium-ion

The safer, tougher alternative to lithium.

Compare chemistries

Inherently safer

Passes crush, short-circuit, over-charge and 130 °C heating abuse tests with no fire and no explosion. Cells can be shipped and stored at 0 V.

Cold-climate ready

Delivers 90% of rated capacity at −20 °C and still discharges at −40 °C. Most sites need no heating blankets or air-con.

Long service life

From 4,000 to over 10,000 full cycles, depending on the model. That's more than 25 years of daily cycling for stationary storage.

Abundant materials

No lithium, cobalt or nickel. Sodium and aluminium supply chains are stable, low-cost and lower in carbon.
