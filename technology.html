Why sodium-ion?

Sodium-ion batteries work like lithium-ion: ions move between a cathode and an anode. But they use one of the most abundant elements on Earth, and that changes the cost, safety and supply story.

1,000×more abundant than lithium in the Earth's crust
90%capacity retained at −20 °C
10,000+cycles on our NX-P160 cell
0 Vsafe shipping & storage state
Safety

Built to stay calm under abuse.

Sodium-ion chemistry releases less heat in failure and is more thermally stable than many lithium chemistries. Our cells are tested well beyond normal use.

  • Over-charge to 5.0 V with no fire and no explosion
  • External short circuit (≤ 5 mΩ, 10 min) with no fire and no explosion
  • Crush, drop, heavy impact and vibration tests passed
  • 130 °C oven test held for 30 minutes with no fire and no explosion
  • −40 °C to 85 °C thermal shock, 32 cycles

Where it fits

When to choose sodium-ion

Stationary storage

Weight doesn't matter on a wall or in a cabinet, but cycle life, safety and cost per cycle do.

Harsh climates

From alpine winters to remote outback enclosures, there's less need for heating or cooling systems.

Lead-acid replacement

Sodium-ion's voltage window maps well to 12/24/48 V systems, with far longer life and less weight.

Supply-chain resilience

Less exposure to lithium price swings and critical-mineral constraints.
